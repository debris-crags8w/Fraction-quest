Privacy

Last updated 30 October 2026 · privacy notice version 1

Fraction Quest is a fraction course for children aged 8 to 10. We save only what learning needs. We don’t show ads, sell data or track children across other apps and websites.

Playing as a guest

Guest progress stays on the device, in the iPhone app too. Nothing about a guest is sent to us.

What a family account saves

A parent or caregiver creates the account and agrees to this notice first. The account saves:

  • the username, the display name, and the password, stored scrambled so no one can read it;
  • the date the parent or caregiver agreed to this notice;
  • each learner’s nickname, lesson answers, progress and app settings;
  • class codes, if a learner joins a class. Class reports show a learner code, never the nickname.

We don’t ask for an email address, a real name, a birthday, a photo or a location. We never record anyone’s voice.

Why we save it

So a child can keep learning where they left off, on another device too, and so a grown-up can see how they are doing. That’s all we use it for.

Read-aloud

When the natural voice reads a lesson, the lesson’s words are sent to our server to make the sound. Nothing the child types or says is sent.

What our server keeps for a short time

Like most websites, our server sees the device’s internet address. We use it to stop abuse, such as too many sign-in attempts, and it can appear in error records that are kept for a short time.

Who can see it

The grown-up who owns the account. A teacher sees only learner codes and progress for their class. The small team that runs Fraction Quest can look at an account’s progress to help a family that asks. We don’t share data with anyone else, except where the law requires it.

Paying

Subscriptions are bought through the App Store or Google Play. They handle the payment. We never see card details.

Seeing and deleting your data

In Grown-ups › Family and privacy settings you can download everything the account saves, or delete the account and all of its learners. A deleted account can’t be brought back.

How long we keep it

Until the account is deleted.

Changes

If we change what we save, we’ll update this page and ask the parent or caregiver to agree again.
